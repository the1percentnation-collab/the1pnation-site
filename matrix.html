You are not bad at social media.
You were handed five platforms, no instructions, and told to be consistent.

So you post when you feel inspired. You go quiet when you do not. You watch people with half your experience build an audience while your best work sits unseen. You have saved a hundred posts about what you are supposed to be doing and you have not used one of them.

And every time you sit down to actually post, the same question stops you cold. Where do I even start.

You have been told the answer is discipline. Post more. Show up daily. Be consistent. So you tried that, and it did not work either, and now you quietly think the problem might be you.

It is not. You are being consistent inside a system whose rules nobody explained. The platforms are not chaos. They are a matrix. Every one of them decides what to show and who to show it to, and every one of them decides differently. Once you can see the rules, the work stops feeling like guessing.

That is not a discipline problem. It is a clarity problem.
Four sessions. You walk out with the rules, and a plan you can actually run.

The Four Sessions

HOW THE ROOM RUNS

Each session is ninety minutes and each one ends with something built, not something to think about later.

01

THE AUDIT

Where you actually stand. How each platform decides what to show and to whom, in plain language. Then you choose the one platform that fits your work and your life, and you let the rest go without guilt. Most people leave this session relieved, because it turns out they were trying to run four races at once.

02

THE MESSAGE

Positioning, voice, and the one sentence everything else hangs on. If the people who already know you cannot repeat what you do, they cannot refer you. We fix that first, because no amount of posting rescues a message nobody can hold onto.

03

THE ENGINE

The four content types, how to batch them, and the weekly rhythm that survives a genuinely busy month. Including the part nobody teaches: exactly what to do on the days you have nothing to say. You will map thirty days before this session ends.

04

THE ASK

Turning attention into conversations, and conversations into clients. How to make an offer without becoming the person nobody wants in their inbox. This is where the audience finally connects to the work you are actually trying to do.
