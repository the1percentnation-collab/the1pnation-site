This policy explains what The One Percent Nation ("we", "us") collects when you use the1pnation.com, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, and optionally your phone number when you join a class interest list, register for a webinar, or contact us. Where a form asks additional questions, your answers to those questions.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We receive a confirmation, the amount, and the last four digits.
  • Usage information. Referring page, marketing campaign parameters in the link you arrived through, and standard server logs including IP address.

Why we collect it

  • To tell you when a class you asked about has been scheduled
  • To deliver a program you enrolled in and give you access to it
  • To send you information about courses, books, masterminds, and webinars, where you have agreed to receive it
  • To understand which content brings people to the site

Text messages

If you opt in to text messages, we may send updates about your inquiry, bookings, or program updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for assistance or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers who make the site work:

  • Google Firebase, for hosting and data storage
  • Stripe, for payment processing
  • Twilio SendGrid, for email delivery
  • GoHighLevel, for customer relationship management

We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you remain on our list, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, tax, or accounting purposes. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom that works immediately
  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving them
  • Email us to request a copy of your information, correct it, or have it deleted
