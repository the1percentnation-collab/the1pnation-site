THE OFFER

This copy renders on the public class page. The flagship page at /matrix uses its own hand-written version, so edits here affect classes shown through /class.html.

SESSIONS

Session titles and descriptions, plus the dates. Dates are what the announcement email and the calendar invite are built from.

SIGNUP FORM

These are the questions on the interest form. Keep the required ones to a minimum. Anything you mark required is friction, and friction at this stage is what costs you signups.

PRICE & SCARCITY

Every number here is enforced on the server. The page cannot promise a rate or a seat the checkout will not honour.

NEW CLASS

Creates a fresh class with the current form fields as a starting point. It goes live at /class.html?c=slug the moment you save.

ANNOUNCE THIS CLASS

This is the second message. It goes to everyone on the interest list and the waitlist with the session dates, the price, a checkout link, an access code field, and a calendar invite. It also flips the class to Scheduled so the public page starts taking payment. Add the session dates first, or this will refuse to send.

PORTAL INSTRUCTIONS

The third message. Goes to everyone who has paid, explaining that class delivery happens in the member portal and how to set up their account. This sends automatically on payment, so use this only to catch someone the automatic send missed.

PORTAL ACCOUNTS

Every signup creates a member portal login in the background and emails the person a link to set their password. Account creation is never allowed to fail a signup, so a bad minute on the portal leaves a lead with no login rather than losing the lead. This goes back and finishes those. Safe to run as often as you like: it skips anyone who already has an account and never touches an existing one.

ACCESS CODES

A code at 100 percent enrols the person directly without a payment, and without the word "free" appearing anywhere on the page, in the checkout, or in their confirmation. The seat keeps its stated value. Codes below 100 percent go through Stripe with the discount applied.
